What this program is

GVO Sound LLC sends one-way text alerts to its own staff when a customer books a studio session on gvosound.com. Recipients are only GVO staff who give their own mobile number to the business to receive these alerts.

What the texts contain

Each alert covers one booking:

  • The session booked
  • The date and time of the session
  • The customer's first name and phone number
  • The amount paid

How staff sign up

Staff opt in verbally. Before a number is added, the GVO Sound manager reads this script to the staff member, in person or on a call:

"GVO Sound LLC can text you an alert each time a customer books a studio session on gvosound.com. It's one text per booking, with the session, the date and time, the customer's first name and phone number, and the amount paid. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop, or HELP for help. Do you agree to get these booking alerts at this number?"

A number is added only after the staff member says yes. The manager records their name, the number and the date they agreed. The first alert they receive includes "Reply STOP to opt out."

How often

One message per booking. Message and data rates may apply.

What this program does not send

No marketing messages. No texts to customers.

Opting out and getting help

Reply STOP to any alert to stop receiving them. Reply HELP for help. You can also reach us at gvosoundllc@gmail.com or (443) 292-6753.
