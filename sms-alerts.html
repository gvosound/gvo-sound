What this program is

GVO Sound LLC sends one-way text alerts to its own staff when a customer books a studio session on gvosound.com. Recipients are only GVO staff who give their own mobile number to the business to receive these alerts.

What the texts contain

Each alert covers one booking:

  • The session booked
  • The date and time of the session
  • The customer's first name and phone number
  • The amount paid

How often

One message per booking. Message and data rates may apply.

What this program does not send

No marketing messages. No texts to customers.

Opting out and getting help

Reply STOP to any alert to stop receiving them. Reply HELP for help. You can also reach us at gvosoundllc@gmail.com or (443) 292-6753.
