Accessibility

Good vibes, open to everyone

gvosound.com should work for you whether you use a screen reader, a keyboard, magnification or voice control, to book a session, buy apparel, find the next party or stream music recorded here.

Last reviewed: November 2026

The standard

We build gvosound.com to WCAG 2.2 Level AA. Every page is tested with automated accessibility checks, then by hand with a keyboard and a screen reader before changes go live.

What's built in

  • A "Skip to content" link is the first thing a keyboard reaches on every page.
  • Everything works from a keyboard, including the studio cards, the booking desk, search, the bag and checkout. Whatever has focus gets a lavender outline.
  • Search, the bag, checkout and pop-ups are announced as dialogs with names, and close with the Escape key.
  • Screen readers hear booking and checkout errors as they happen, and the studio artist picker works as a set of tabs.
  • Photos carry text descriptions. Video clips are silent, so no sound plays without you choosing it.
  • Text meets AA contrast against GVO's dark background.
  • If your device is set to reduce motion, the homepage and studio videos are replaced with still photos and the animations stop.
  • On phones, a bar at the bottom calls or texts the studio in one tap, and addresses open directions in Google Maps.
  • Forms say exactly what's missing, like "Add your street address," and move you to that field.

Still being worked on

  • Some content comes from other companies and works the way they built it: Square's card payment fields, Instagram posts, YouTube sessions, Spotify and Apple Music players, Eventbrite tickets and Google Maps.
  • The studio walkthrough and session clips have no captions or audio description. They're silent, and each studio page describes the room, the gear and the rates in text.
  • Pins on the travel map are small. The list of places under the map opens each one, and works with a keyboard.

Something not working?

Tell us what happened and where on the site. We'll help you book a session, place an order or get what you came for another way.
