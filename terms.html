These terms cover studio bookings and shop orders on gvosound.com, and the GVO Sound booking alerts text program. GVO Sound LLC runs the site and the studio at 109 Gibralter Ave, Annapolis, MD 21401.

Studio bookings

  • You pay the full session price when you book. That holds your time slot.
  • Sessions book at least 2 hours ahead.
  • Up to 5 people per session.
  • Maryland sales tax of 6% is added at checkout.

Cancellations and changes

Full price is paid up front, and there are no refunds. To switch to another time, call or text (443) 292-6753 no later than 2 hours before your session.

Shop orders

  • Shipping in the US is $9.99. International shipping is $15.99. Orders ship in 3 to 5 business days.
  • Pickup at the studio is free. Your order waits at 109 Gibralter Ave, and the studio lets you know when it is ready.
  • You choose shipping or pickup at checkout.
