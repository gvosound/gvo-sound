Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Who we are

This site, gvosound.com, is run by GVO Sound LLC, 109 Gibralter Ave, Annapolis, MD 21401.

What the site collects

  • Booking and order details: your name, email, phone number, and what you booked or bought.
  • What you send through the site's forms, such as quote requests, event inquiries, travel inquiries and music submissions.
  • Payment details, which go straight to Square for processing. The site never sees or stores full card numbers.

How it is used

  • To provide your booking or order, and to reply to what you send us.
  • To send booking and order confirmations.
  • To send internal alerts to GVO Sound staff when a booking or order comes in.

Service providers

These companies handle parts of the work for us:

  • Square: payments and bookings
  • Google: form storage and email
  • Resend: email delivery
  • Twilio: text alerts to GVO Sound staff
  • Cloudflare: hosting and spam protection

Text messaging

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

The only texts this site's systems send are booking alerts to GVO Sound staff. See the SMS terms for how that program works.

Deleting or correcting your information

To ask us to delete or correct information we hold about you, email gvosoundllc@gmail.com.
